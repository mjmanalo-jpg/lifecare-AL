Orientation

Screen basics — the same controls everywhere

Once you know these, every portal feels familiar. They behave identically across all 15 roles.

The left sidebar

Your tools, organized into collapsible groups (Overview, Clinical Monitoring, Resident Care, Operations, and so on). Click a group heading to expand or collapse it; click any item to open it. The ‹ button at the top collapses the whole sidebar to icons.

The top bar

Company selector (left) switches facility if you manage more than one. The bell shows your notifications — automated alerts land here. Your profile menu (right) holds Settings, Change Password and Log out.

Buttons & confirmations

Primary actions are the solid blue buttons (Save, Create, Assign). Anything destructive asks first in a pop-up with a red icon — Confirm or Cancel. After an action a small toast slides in bottom-right: green = done, red = something went wrong (with the reason).

Lists, search & export

Most screens have a search box, filters (priority, status, date range) and paging. Where a report exists, an Export CSV button downloads exactly what you're viewing.

Automated alerts. LCMS watches the data and raises alerts on its own — abnormal vitals, missed medications, overdue tasks & follow-ups, low stock, and weight loss — delivering them to the right team's notification bell. No one has to go looking.

Do it

Key workflows, step by step

The everyday tasks that cross roles — where to click and what happens.

Assign & complete a care task

  1. Nurse assigns. /nurse → Task Assignment → Assign Task → pick the resident, the caregiver, priority and due time → Add Task.
  2. Caregiver sees it. /caregiver → Task Assignment shows the checklist, newest first. Tick the box to mark it complete.
  3. If it slips. An overdue task auto-alerts the nurse & facility admin in their notification bell.

Give a medication (MAR)

  1. Open the record. /nurse → Medication Administration Record.
  2. Log the dose. Mark it Given, or Refused / Held — a reason is required for the last two, plus a witness where needed.
  3. Missed doses. A scheduled dose left un-given for 30+ minutes flips to Missed and alerts the team automatically.

Escalate a clinical concern (SBAR)

  1. Raise it. /nurse → Clinical Coordination → new escalation. Fill Situation, Background (auto-filled from history), Assessment, Recommendation, and set priority.
  2. Physician responds. /physician → Clinical Coordination — acknowledge, reply with orders, resolve. The SLA clock turns red if it breaches.

Endorse a shift

  1. Check what's open. /nurse → Shift Endorsement — the Carry-Over & Unresolved panel lists open escalations, pending/overdue tasks and due follow-ups with priority and time.
  2. Write & sign. New Report → fill the handover → Sign to validate it. It appears instantly to the incoming shift.

Bill a resident and send the invoice

  1. Gather charges. /billing_admin → Transactions Ledger — tick a resident's pending charges → Generate Invoice.
  2. Issue it. Set the due date, then Create & Send. The invoice is marked Sent and the resident + family get a bell notification and can view/pay it in their portal.

Restock inventory (purchase request)

  1. Raise a request. /facility_admin → Purchase Requests → New Request (link an inventory item to prefill), set quantity, cost and priority → Submit.
  2. Move it along. Approve → Mark Ordered → Mark Received. Receiving automatically adds the quantity back to stock on hand.
