# FAQ & How-To

## For residents and families
**Q: How do I talk to the AI assistant?**
Use the mic button to talk, or type your question. You can ask about your schedule, meals, activities, appointments, or how you're doing.

**Q: Where do I see my appointments?**
Your dashboard shows your appointment calendar. Meetings you schedule with family or doctors also appear on your family member's calendar.

**Q: How do I sign a document?**
Open the family or resident portal, go to your documents/forms, review the form, and use the on-screen signature to sign. You can also upload documents.

**Q: When are meals and activities?**
Meal times, daily menus, and the events calendar are managed in the system. Ask the assistant or check your dashboard for the current schedule. (Facility-specific times can be added to the assistant's "Extra instructions" or to this knowledge base.)

## For staff
**Q: How do I record a daily round?**
Open Daily Rounds, select the resident, choose the shift, and fill the ten documentation areas. Save — it reflects on the resident profile.

**Q: How do I raise an escalation?**
Use the SBAR escalation form: fill Situation, Background, Assessment, Recommendation, set a priority, and submit. It routes to the physician and appears in the Alert Center.

**Q: How do I approve a medication?**
Physicians/Administrator/Care Manager open Medication Approvals, review the pending order, and approve. Approved orders become active for the MAR.

**Q: How do I dispense from inventory correctly?**
Use FEFO — the dispense screen previews the batch nearest to expiry first. Select that batch to avoid waste and expiries.

**Q: Where is my signing PIN?**
Open Settings in your portal; your 4-digit signing PIN is shown there. Use it to sign and lock records like shift reports.

**Q: How do I find a resident quickly?**
Scan the resident's QR card, which carries their Patient ID and key profile details.

**Q: Why can't I edit a signed record?**
Signed records are locked. Only allowed follow-up fields can be changed after signing; the rest is protected for compliance.

## Training the assistant
To improve answers, upload documents to the Knowledge Base (any text, Markdown, CSV, PDF, or image with text). Keep facility-specific details — meal times, visiting hours, house rules — either in these documents or in the assistant's "Extra instructions" field. The assistant answers from what is in the knowledge base.
