# Roles and Portals

Every user signs into a portal matched to their role. A user's community role decides which portal and features they see.

## Administrative & oversight
- **Administrator (Super Admin)** — Full system access: system-wide oversight, approvals, audit log (records ALL transactions in the system), staff registries, feature matrix, AI Assistant configuration, and the Landing Studio. This is the top-level operator role.
- **Organization Admin** — SaaS/tenant management: manages communities, memberships, invitations, and organization-level settings across the organization.
- **Care Manager** — Clinical oversight portal, split out from Facility Operations. Owns clinical approvals, incidents, the Alert Center, daily rounds, assessment & acuity, care planning, MAR oversight, referrals, physician coordination, follow-ups, clinical reports, consent & move-in forms, and a Care Activity Log (its audit view is scoped to clinical activity: task completions, MAR, daily rounds, etc.).
- **Facility Operations (Facility Admin)** — Runs non-clinical operations of the community: staffing, hospitality, maintenance, and general facility administration. Clinical functions were moved to the Care Manager role.
- **Billing & Finance (Billing Admin)** — Dedicated billing portal in the Admin & Billing domain: charges, invoices, statements, payments, disputes, and accounting exports.

## Clinical staff
- **Physician** — Reviews and approves medication orders, co-signs notes, responds to escalations, and manages care directives and consults.
- **Nurse** — Frontline clinical care: MAR administration, daily rounds, vitals, incidents, escalations (SBAR), and alert response.
- **Caregiver** — Daily assistance and documentation: tasks, rounds contributions, and resident support. Sees assigned tasks and notes.

## Residents & families
- **Resident** — Personal dashboard: schedule, meals, vitals, documents to sign, appointments, and the AI companion.
- **Family** — Views their resident's status, signs/uploads documents, and sees a shared appointment calendar (meetings the resident schedules appear here).

## Operations & services
- **Fleet Management** — Vehicles, drivers, transport requests, trips, maintenance, and fuel logs.
- **Driver** — Assigned trips and transport tasks.
- **Security** — Front-desk visits, monitoring support, and access oversight.
- **Nutritionist** — Menus, dietitian consults, and food compliance.
- **Kitchen** — Meal preparation and dining service.
- **Housekeeping** — Room turnovers and cleaning schedules.
- **Maintenance** — Facility maintenance calendar and service requests.

## Platform
- **Platform Admin / Support / Auditor** — The software provider's roles for cross-organization support and platform governance (outside any single community).
