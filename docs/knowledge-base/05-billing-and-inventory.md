# Billing, Finance & Inventory

## Billing & Finance
All amounts are shown in **Philippine Peso (₱)** system-wide. The Billing & Finance portal (Admin & Billing domain) covers:
- **Charge library** — reusable charge/service definitions.
- **Invoices & recurring accrual** — invoices are generated and recurring charges accrue automatically on a schedule.
- **Account statements** — per-resident/payer statements of charges and payments.
- **Payments & auto-pay** — payment recording and automatic payment support. Online card payments are built but gated off until a payment provider is approved.
- **Disputes** — billing disputes can be raised and tracked.
- **GL / accounting export** — general-ledger export to QuickBooks and Sage formats.
- **Insurance validation** — insurance checks recorded against residents.

## Inventory & Materials
The inventory module manages stock and assets:
- **Items with auto-generated barcodes** — every inventory item gets an auto-generated batch/lot number and a real, scannable **UPC-A barcode** (with a valid check digit), shown when viewing the item.
- **FEFO dispensing (First-Expire-First-Out)** — when dispensing, the system previews the batch nearest to expiry first so staff select the correct lot and reduce waste.
- **Barcode scanning** — items can be looked up by scanning their barcode.
- **Vendors** — supplier records for purchasing.
- **Purchase requests** — a purchase-request workflow for restocking.
- **Asset maintenance** — tracks maintainable assets and their service schedules.
- **Inventory alerts** — low-stock and expiry alerts.
