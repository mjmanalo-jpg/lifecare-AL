# Clinical Care Workflows

## Daily Rounds
Bedside documentation performed each shift (DAY / EVENING / NIGHT) across ten documentation areas, including meals/nutrition, fluids, elimination (bowel/urine), mobility, sleep, mood, pain, skin/edema, medication notes, and general concerns. Rounds are visible across the nurse, caregiver, physician, and Care Manager portals. Each round is tied to a resident; a resident must be selected before a round can be saved.

## Assessment & Level of Care (Acuity)
The Assessment & Acuity board scores residents across nine care dimensions to determine their level of care (Independent, Assisted, Memory, or Skilled). Completing an assessment syncs the resident's care level on their profile.

## Care Plans
Care plans capture goals and care-plan items per resident, coordinating the interventions the care team follows.

## Medication Administration Record (MAR)
The MAR tracks each medication administration event. See "Medication Management" for the ordering and approval flow.

## Vitals
Vital signs (blood pressure, heart rate, temperature, oxygen/SpO2, blood glucose, weight, respiratory rate) are recorded and trend over time; abnormal readings can drive escalations and alerts.

## Incidents
Incidents (falls, injuries, behavioral events, etc.) are reported with follow-up notes. Severe or critical incidents automatically raise an alert to the Care Manager and nurses in the Alert Center with an SLA countdown.

## Escalations (SBAR)
Clinical concerns are escalated using the SBAR format — Situation, Background, Assessment, Recommendation — with a priority (ROUTINE, URGENT, EMERGENCY). Escalations are routed to the physician, acknowledged, responded to, and resolved. Response-time targets (SLA) apply per severity.

## Follow-ups and Referrals
- **Follow-up Tracker** — tracks pending clinical follow-up actions to completion.
- **Referrals** — hospital and specialist referrals, which the Care Manager can approve.

## Physician Coordination
Physicians review pending medication orders, co-sign notes, log physician communications, and respond to escalations.

## Clinical alerts and automation
The Alert Center surfaces time-sensitive clinical events with SLA response windows by severity. An alerts-automation engine watches for triggers (e.g. severe incidents, abnormal vitals) and raises alerts, deduplicating so the same event does not fire twice. A shift-continuity checker helps ensure handovers are complete.
