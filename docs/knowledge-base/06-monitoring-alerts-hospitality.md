# Monitoring, Alerts, Hospitality & CRM

## Camera monitoring & safety
The monitoring system is designed for CCTV (migrating from webcam), using body- and pose-based detection so it works even when a subject is far away or not facing the camera:
- **Person-ROI zoom** — zooms into the detected person so far-away subjects are still analyzed.
- **Pre-fall alarms** — bed-exit and abrupt/aggressive-wake detection trigger early warnings before a fall.
- **Fall and inactivity detection** — flags falls and unusual stillness.
- **Sleep / eye-closure detection** — uses facial landmarks (eye-blink) to detect sleep and drowsiness.
- **Emotion detection** — real-time facial emotion read from pose landmarks.
- A **Camera Activity Log** records monitoring events.

## Alert Center
The Alert Center is the hub for time-sensitive events (severe incidents, abnormal vitals, bed-exit, SLA breaches). Each alert has a response-time target (SLA) by severity and can be acknowledged, snoozed, or resolved. Administrator, Care Manager, and Nurse have full control; other staff can acknowledge.

## Hospitality & PMS (hotel-style services)
Golden Hearth runs hotel-style resident services:
- **Service requests & concierge bookings** — residents/families request services and book concierge items.
- **Front desk visits** — visitor check-in/out at the front desk.
- **Dining reservations & daily menus** — meal planning and reservations, with dietitian consults and food-compliance logs.
- **Room turnovers & housekeeping** — cleaning and turnover scheduling.
- **Community events & announcements** — activities calendar, event attendance, and facility announcements.
- **Facility maintenance calendar** — scheduled maintenance and service requests.
- **Resident preferences** — captured to personalize service.

## CRM & Move-In pipeline
A CRM lead pipeline tracks prospective residents from inquiry through move-in. An in-app **appointment calendar** lets residents and families schedule meetings (with family, doctors, tours, etc.); when a resident schedules a meeting, it appears on the family member's calendar too.
